# MediSync AI

### AI-Powered Clinical Documentation & Patient History Management System

MediSync AI is a web-based healthcare management system designed to connect **Patients, Doctors, and Receptionists** through a single digital platform.

The system helps manage **patient registration, appointments, medical records, diagnostic reports, and patient history**. It also provides **voice-to-text assistance** to help doctors with clinical documentation.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of MediSync AI is to digitize and simplify healthcare workflows by bringing patient registration, appointment management, clinical documentation, medical reports, and patient history into one centralized system.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔐 Role-based authentication
- 👤 Patient registration and login
- 🆔 Permanent Patient ID
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor management
- 🧑‍💼 Receptionist management
- 📅 Appointment booking and scheduling
- 📝 Digital medical records
- 🎤 Voice-to-text clinical documentation
- 📄 Medical report upload and management
- 🕒 Patient timeline
- 🔎 Patient search
- ☁️ Firebase cloud storage
- 🔒 Secure password handling

---

#  User Modules

##  Patient Module

Patients can:

- Register and log in
- View their Permanent Patient ID
- Browse available doctors
- Book appointments
- Select appointment slots
- Enter symptoms
- View appointment details
- View medical history
- View and download medical reports
- View their complete Patient Timeline

---

##  Doctor Module

Doctors can:

- Register and log in
- View scheduled appointments
- View patient information
- Access patient medical history
- Add diagnosis
- Add doctor's notes
- Add prescriptions
- Use voice-to-text for clinical documentation
- Upload medical reports
- View patient timeline

---

##  Receptionist Module

Receptionists can:

- Register and log in
- Register new patients
- Generate Permanent Patient IDs
- Search patients using:
  - Name
  - Patient ID
  - Phone
  - Email
- Schedule appointments
- Manage appointment status
- Register walk-in patients
- Upload diagnostic reports
- Access patient information and timeline
